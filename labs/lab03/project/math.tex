\section{Inline and display mathematics}

The formula is now displayed separately:
\[
y = mx + c
\]

The same formula in display mode:
\[
y = mx + c
\]

Superscripts and subscripts:
$a^{b}$, $a_{b}$, $x^{2}$, $x_{i}$.

Greek letters and mathematical functions:
$\alpha$, $\beta$, $\theta$, $\Gamma$, $\Delta$,
$y = 2\sin\theta^{2}$, $\log x$.

\section{Numbered equation}

An integral with an automatically generated number:
\begin{equation}
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^{2}} \, dx
\end{equation}

\section{Aligned equations}

A recurrence without equation numbers:
\begin{align*}
Q_{n,0} &= 1 \quad Q_{0,k} = [k=0]; \\
Q_{n,k} &= Q_{n-1,k}+Q_{n-1,k-1}+\binom{n}{k},
           \quad\text{for $n$, $k>0$.}
\end{align*}

Several columns of aligned equations:
\begin{align*}
a &= b+1   & c &= d+2   & e &= f+3 \\
r &= s^{2} & t &= u^{3} & v &= w^{4}
\end{align*}

\section{Matrices}

Three matrix styles:
\[
\begin{matrix}
a & b & c \\
d & e & f
\end{matrix}
\quad
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b & c \\
d & e & f
\end{pmatrix}
\quad
\begin{bmatrix}
a & b & c \\
d & e & f
\end{bmatrix}
\]

Right-aligned matrix columns using mathtools:
\[
\begin{pmatrix*}[r]
10 & 11 \\
1 & 2 \\
-5 & -6
\end{pmatrix*}
\]

\section{Other multiline environments}

Centered equations without alignment:
\begin{gather}
P(x)=ax^{5}+bx^{4}+cx^{3}+dx^{2}+ex+f \\
x^{2}+x=10
\end{gather}

A single expression split across several lines:
\begin{multline*}
(a+b+c+d)x^{5}+(b+c+d+e)x^{4} \\
+(c+d+e+f)x^{3}+(d+e+f+a)x^{2}+(e+f+a+b)x \\
+(f+a+b+c)
\end{multline*}

An aligned block inside a larger expression:
\[
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
a &= b \\
c &= d
\end{aligned}
\right.
\]

\section{Bold mathematics}

Normal weight:
$(x+y)(x-y)=x^{2}-y^{2}$.

A bold expression:
{\boldmath $(x+y)(x-y)=x^{2}-y^{2}$}.

Individual bold Latin letters:
$(x+\mathbf{y})(x-\mathbf{y})=x^{2}-\mathbf{y}^{2}$.

Bold Greek letters using bm:
$\alpha+\bm{\alpha}<\beta+\bm{\beta}$.
